# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Admin**:
A Cadence staff member who runs the platform. Only an Admin decides a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: the team, staff (for this role), Team Admin

**Team Admin**:
A learner who manages the seats of a team licence. A Team Admin is not an Admin and cannot decide a Refund Request.

### Courses

**Course Access**:
A learner's right to watch and read a course's content.
_Avoid_: Enrollment (for the right itself), Entitlement

**Seat**:
One place on a team licence, claimed with a code. A Seat that nobody has claimed yet is an unclaimed Seat.
_Avoid_: Coupon (for the place itself), Licence (for one place)

**Progress**:
How far a learner has got through a course: the time they have watched and the lessons they have completed. Progress belongs to the learner, so it outlives their Course Access.
_Avoid_: Completion (for the whole measure)

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. An Admin approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once. After a learner has an Approved Refund Request for a course, they cannot ask for a refund on that course again.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin hasn't decided yet. The learner keeps Course Access while it is open.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund, and it ends the learner's Course Access at that moment. Their Progress is kept and comes back if they buy the course again. For a team licence, approval also makes every unclaimed Seat unusable.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has turned down, with a reason the learner can see. It has no Refund, the learner keeps Course Access, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund an Admin has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.
